# Frequently Asked Questions

## Is Flashboard free?
Yes, basic features are free. Pro features require a subscription.

## Does it support multiple languages?
Currently, we support English, Spanish, and French. More coming soon!

## My keyboard isn't showing up?
Try restarting the background service or checking your OS permissions.
