# Installation Guide

Welcome to Flashboard! Follow these steps to get started on your Android device.

<Accordion>
<AccordionItem title="System Requirements">
- **Android Version**: Android 8.0 (API level 26) or higher
- **RAM**: 2GB minimum (4GB+ recommended for optimal performance)
- **Storage**: 50MB free space
- **Internet**: Required for initial download and updates
</AccordionItem>

<AccordionItem title="Step 1: Download from Google Play Store">
1. Open the **Google Play Store** app on your Android device
2. Search for **"Flashboard Keyboard"**
3. Tap the **Install** button
4. Wait for the installation to complete
</AccordionItem>

<AccordionItem title="Step 2: Grant Permissions">
After installation, Flashboard will request the following permissions:
- **Input method permission**: Required to function as a keyboard
- **Microphone** (optional): For voice input feature
- **Contacts** (optional): For smart contact suggestions
- **Storage** (conditional): For custom theme files

Tap **Allow** to proceed with each permission.
</AccordionItem>

<AccordionItem title="Step 3: Enable Flashboard as Your Default Keyboard">
1. Go to **Settings** > **System** > **Languages & Input** (or **Input Methods**)
2. Tap **On-screen keyboard** or **Virtual keyboard**
3. Select **Flashboard Keyboard**
4. Follow the setup wizard to complete activation

> **Note**: Different Android manufacturers may have slightly different settings paths. Check [Android keyboard switching guide](https://android.com/intl/en_us/accessibility/quick-start/switch-keyboard/) if you need help.
</AccordionItem>

<AccordionItem title="Step 4: Switch to Flashboard">
When typing in any app, press and hold the **space bar** or tap the **keyboard icon**, then select **Flashboard Keyboard** from the available keyboards.
</AccordionItem>
