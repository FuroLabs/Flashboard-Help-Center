# First Launch Setup

When you open Flashboard for the first time, you'll see a setup wizard. Here's what each step covers.

<Accordion>
<AccordionItem title="Welcome Screen">
You'll see:
- Flashboard logo and welcome message
- Quick overview of key features
- **"Let's Get Started"** button

Tap **"Let's Get Started"** to proceed.
</AccordionItem>

<AccordionItem title="Step 1: Input Method Permission">
Flashboard needs permission to be your keyboard:

**What you'll see**:
- "Flashboard Keyboard wants to be your input method"
- Settings button or permission dialog

**What to do**:
- Tap **"OK"** or **"Allow"**
- You may be redirected to Android Settings
- In Settings, select **Flashboard Keyboard** and confirm
</AccordionItem>

<AccordionItem title="Step 2: Permissions Grant">
Flashboard will request optional permissions:

**Required Permissions**
- ✅ **Input method** - Mandatory for keyboard function

**Optional Permissions**
- 🎙️ **Microphone** - For voice input feature
- 📱 **Contacts** - For smart suggestions
- 📂 **Files/Storage** - For custom themes and backups

**You can always enable these later** in Settings > App Permissions.

For privacy-conscious users, Flashboard works great with just the input method permission.
</AccordionItem>

<AccordionItem title="Step 3: Keyboard Selection">
**"Set Flashboard as your default keyboard?"**

- **Tap "Yes"** - Flashboard becomes your main keyboard (recommended)
- **Tap "Later"** - You can manually switch keyboards when typing

If you choose "Later", see [Enabling Flashboard Keyboard](/help/setup/enabling-keyboard) for manual activation.
</AccordionItem>

<AccordionItem title="Step 4: Language & Region">
Select your preferences:
- **Language**: Your typing language
- **Region**: Affects dictionary and suggestions
- **Text Correction**: Enable/disable autocorrect

You can change these anytime in [Settings](/help/customization/settings).
</AccordionItem>

<AccordionItem title="Step 5: Theme Selection">
Choose your preferred keyboard appearance:
- **Light Theme** - White/light gray background
- **Dark Theme** - Black/dark background
- **High Contrast** - Bold colors for accessibility

See [Customizing Themes](/help/customization/themes) to explore more themes later.
</AccordionItem>

<AccordionItem title="Step 6: Completion">
**"All Set!"** screen shows:
- ✅ Flashboard is now active
- Quick link to explore features
- Open in-app preferences

Tap **"Start Typing"** or close to begin using Flashboard!
</AccordionItem>

<AccordionItem title="What's Next?">
**Explore These Guides:**
- [Gesture Controls](/help/features/gestures) - Master swipe typing
- [Voice Input](/help/features/voice-input) - Type with your voice
- [Themes & Customization](/help/customization/themes) - Personalize your keyboard
- [Settings Guide](/help/customization/settings) - Fine-tune behavior

**First-time Tips:**
1. **Try gesture typing**: Swipe across keys to form words
2. **Use voice typing**: Tap 🎙️ to speak instead of type
3. **Experiment with themes**: Swipe left in keyboard menu
4. **Check out gesture shortcuts**: Long-press or multi-finger swipes
</AccordionItem>

<AccordionItem title="Skip Setup Later?">
If you accidentally closed the wizard, you can restart it:
1. Open the **Flashboard app**
2. Tap **Settings** > **About**
3. Tap **"Run Setup Wizard"**
</AccordionItem>

<AccordionItem title="Troubleshooting First Setup">
**Permissions not working?**
- Go to **Settings > Apps > Flashboard > Permissions**
- Ensure each permission shows "Allowed"
- Restart the Flashboard app

**Can't find keyboard after setup?**
- See [Enabling Flashboard Keyboard](/help/setup/enabling-keyboard) troubleshooting section
- May need to manually enable in Android Settings

**Want to reset all settings?**
- Open Flashboard app > **Settings > Advanced > Reset to Defaults**
- This will restart the setup wizard
</AccordionItem>
</Accordion>
