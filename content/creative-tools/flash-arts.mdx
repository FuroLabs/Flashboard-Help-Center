# Flash Arts

Express yourself with creative ASCII art, emoticons, kaomoji, and decorative text art. Flash Arts brings a huge collection of expressive characters right to your keyboard.

<Accordion>
<AccordionItem title="Access Flash Arts">
1. Open any app where you can type
2. Bring up the Flashboard keyboard
3. Tap the **😊 Emoji button** (usually bottom-left or next to spacebar)
4. In the emoji panel, tap the **middle tab/icon** (Flash Arts)
5. Browse through categories or search for art

**Navigation:**
- **Left tab** - Standard emojis
- **Middle tab** - Flash Arts ✨ (ASCII art & emoticons)
- **Right tab** - GIFs (if enabled)
</AccordionItem>

<AccordionItem title="Use Flash Arts">
### Insert Art into Text

1. Open Flash Arts via Emoji button > Middle tab
2. Browse or search for the art you want
3. Tap to insert at cursor position
4. Continue typing your message

### Search Flash Arts

1. Open Flash Arts
2. Tap the **🔍 search icon**
3. Type keywords: "happy", "cat", "arrow", "heart"
4. Browse results
5. Tap to insert

**Popular Search Terms:**
- Emotions: happy, sad, angry, love, laugh
- Animals: cat, dog, bear, bunny
- Actions: shrug, wave, dance, hug
- Objects: star, heart, arrow, music

### Save Favorites

Like what you see? Save it for later:

1. Long-press any Flash Art
2. Tap **⭐ Add to Favorites**
3. Access favorites from the **Favorites tab** at the top

Remove from favorites:
1. Go to **Favorites tab**
2. Long-press the art
3. Tap **Remove from Favorites**
</AccordionItem>

<AccordionItem title="Flash Arts Settings">
Customize your experience:

1. Open **Flashboard app** > **Settings** > **Flash Arts**
2. Adjust these options:

**Enable Flash Arts**
- Toggle to show/hide Flash Arts in emoji panel

**Recent Arts**
- Show recently used arts for quick access

**Favorites Count**
- Set maximum number of favorite arts

**Art Categories**
- Enable/disable specific categories

**Search Suggestions**
- Show popular search terms
</AccordionItem>

<AccordionItem title="Create Custom Flash Arts">
Save your own ASCII art creations:

1. Open **Flashboard app** > **Flash Arts** > **My Arts**
2. Tap **+ Create New**
3. Enter your ASCII art or emoticon
4. Add a name and tags (for searching)
5. Tap **Save**
6. It now appears in your Flash Arts collection

**Tips for Creating:**
- Use simple characters that work on all devices
- Test in different apps before saving
- Keep it concise for easy use
- Add descriptive tags for easy searching
</AccordionItem>

<AccordionItem title="Troubleshooting">
### Can't find Flash Arts tab?
- Tap the **😊 Emoji button** to open emoji panel
- Look for tabs at the top or bottom of the panel
- Flash Arts is typically the **middle tab**
- Ensure Flash Arts is enabled in settings

### Characters appear as boxes?
- The receiving device may not support these Unicode characters
- Try simpler ASCII art
- Update the receiving device/app
- Some very decorative characters may not display everywhere

### Flash Arts not saving?
- Check storage permissions for Flashboard
- Try clearing app cache
- Update Flashboard to latest version

### Search not finding anything?
- Try different keywords
- Check spelling
- Browse categories instead
- Some arts may not have search tags yet
</AccordionItem>
