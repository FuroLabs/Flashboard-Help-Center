# Gesture Controls

Flashboard transforms your typing experience with intuitive gestures. Swipe, slide, and tap your way to faster, more efficient typing.

<Accordion>
<AccordionItem title="Essential Gestures">
### Swipe Typing (Glide Typing)

Type words by swiping across letters:

1. **Place your finger on the first letter** of the word
2. **Slide continuously across each letter** without lifting
3. **Lift your finger after the last letter**
4. The word appears automatically

**Tips:**
- Don't worry about precision - Flashboard predicts your intent
- Space is added automatically after each word
- Works in all supported languages

### Cursor Control

Move the cursor precisely without blocking your view:

**Method 1: Spacebar Slide**
1. **Slide your finger left/right on the spacebar**
2. Cursor moves character by character
3. Lift finger to stop

**Method 2: Keyboard Surface Slide** (if enabled)
1. **Long-press anywhere on the keyboard** until it becomes translucent
2. **Slide your finger** to move the cursor
3. Lift to exit cursor mode

### Quick Delete

Delete words and text faster:

**Delete Word**
1. **Swipe left with two fingers** on the keyboard
2. Deletes the previous word instantly

**Delete Character**
1. **Swipe left on the backspace key** for continuous deletion
2. Or tap repeatedly for single characters

**Delete Line**
1. **Long-press backspace**
2. Continues deleting until released
</AccordionItem>

<AccordionItem title="Text Selection Gestures">
### Select Text

**Method 1: Two-Finger Slide**
1. **Place two fingers on the keyboard**
2. **Slide left** to select backwards
3. **Slide right** to select forwards

**Method 2: Long-Press Space + Slide**
1. **Long-press spacebar**
2. **Slide left/right** to select text
3. Lift to complete selection

### Select Word
1. **Double-tap on the spacebar**
2. The previous word is selected

### Select All
1. **Triple-tap the text field** (in some apps)
2. Or use **long-press spacebar** and slide to ends
</AccordionItem>

<AccordionItem title="Advanced Gestures">
### Emoji Quick Access

**Method 1:**
1. **Long-press the Enter/Return key**
2. Emoji picker opens instantly

**Method 2:**
1. **Swipe up from spacebar** (if enabled in gestures settings)
2. Opens emoji panel

### Symbol Access

**Quick Symbol Insert:**
1. **Long-press any letter key**
2. Common symbols and accented characters appear
3. Slide to the symbol and release

**Numbers Row:**
1. **Swipe down on the top letter row** (if enabled)
2. Reveals numbers without switching keyboards

### Voice Input

1. **Long-press the spacebar** or tap the microphone icon
2. Speak your text
3. Tap again to stop
4. Works in all supported languages

### One-Handed Mode

For easier thumb typing:

1. **Long-press the comma (,) or period (.) key**
2. Select arrow to shift keyboard left or right
3. Keyboard compacts to one side
4. Tap the arrow to return to full size

### Capital Letters & Shift

**Single Capital:**
1. **Tap Shift** once
2. Next letter is capitalized

**Caps Lock:**
1. **Double-tap Shift**
2. All letters capitalized until you tap Shift again

**Quick Capital (No Shift):**
1. **Swipe up on any letter key**
2. Types the capital letter instantly

### Quick Punctuation

**Fast Period:**
1. **Double-tap spacebar**
2. Adds period + space + capital next letter

**Quick Symbols:**
1. **Long-press punctuation keys** (. , ? !)
2. Reveals related symbols
3. Slide to select
</AccordionItem>

<AccordionItem title="Gesture Settings">
### Enable/Disable Gestures

1. Open **Flashboard app** > **Settings** > **Gestures**
2. Toggle individual gestures on/off:
   - Swipe typing
   - Spacebar cursor control
   - Two-finger delete
   - Emoji quick access
   - Symbol shortcuts

### Customize Gesture Sensitivity

1. Go to **Settings** > **Gestures** > **Sensitivity**
2. Adjust:
   - **Swipe sensitivity** - How much movement triggers a swipe
   - **Long-press duration** - How long to hold for long-press actions
   - **Cursor speed** - How fast cursor moves during slide

### Remap Gesture Actions

Create custom gesture shortcuts:

1. Open **Settings** > **Gestures** > **Custom Actions**
2. Select a gesture trigger:
   - Swipe up on spacebar
   - Swipe down on spacebar
   - Three-finger swipe
   - Long-press Enter
3. Assign action:
   - Open clipboard
   - Insert signature
   - Switch language
   - Open settings
   - Custom text paste
</AccordionItem>

<AccordionItem title="Platform-Specific Gestures">
### Samsung Devices

Additional gesture on Samsung keyboards:

**Keyboard Resize:**
1. **Pinch with two fingers** on the keyboard
2. Drag to resize height
3. Release to set

### Large Screen Devices (Tablets)

**Split Keyboard:**
1. **Long-press keyboard mode icon** (⌨️)
2. Select **Split Keyboard**
3. Keyboard divides for two-thumb typing
4. Pinch together to return to full keyboard
</AccordionItem>

<AccordionItem title="Gesture Tips & Tricks">
**Fastest Typing:**
- Combine swipe typing with spacebar cursor control
- Use two-finger delete for quick corrections
- Enable gesture trail for visual feedback

**Accuracy:**
- Slow down swipe typing for better word recognition
- Use tap typing for complex or unique words
- Combine both methods as needed

**Comfort:**
- Enable one-handed mode on large phones
- Adjust keyboard height in Settings
- Use floating keyboard for multitasking

**Learning:**
- Practice swipe typing with common words first
- Gestures improve with muscle memory
- Check out in-app gesture tutorial: **Settings** > **Help** > **Gesture Tutorial**
</AccordionItem>

<AccordionItem title="Troubleshooting Gestures">
### Swipe typing not working?
- Enable in **Settings** > **Gestures** > **Swipe Typing**
- Check if your language supports swipe input
- Clear keyboard cache: **Settings** > **Advanced** > **Clear Cache**

### Cursor control too sensitive?
- Adjust in **Settings** > **Gestures** > **Sensitivity**
- Reduce cursor speed
- Increase touch precision in Android accessibility settings

### Gestures triggering accidentally?
- Disable specific gestures in settings
- Increase gesture sensitivity threshold
- Enable palm rejection: **Settings** > **Gestures** > **Palm Rejection**

### Quick delete removing too much?
- Adjust deletion speed in settings
- Use single-tap backspace instead
- Toggle off two-finger delete gesture
</AccordionItem>
