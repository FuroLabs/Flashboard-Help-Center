# Clipboard Manager

Flashboard's clipboard manager automatically saves everything you copy, making it easy to access and reuse text snippets anytime.

## How Clipboard Works

Every time you copy text on your device, Flashboard automatically saves it to your clipboard history - no manual action needed.

**Auto-Save Feature:**
- Copies are saved instantly when you use copy/cut
- Works across all apps on your device
- Stores up to 50 recent items (configurable in settings)
- Clips persist even after restarting your device

<AccordionItem title="Access & Use Clipboard History">

### Access Clipboard History

1. Open any app where you can type
2. Tap on a text field to bring up Flashboard
3. Tap the **📋 clipboard icon** in the top toolbar
4. Your clipboard history appears

### Paste a Clip

1. Open the **clipboard manager** (📋 icon)
2. Scroll through your saved clips
3. Tap any clip to paste it into your text field

### Pin Important Clips

1. Open the **clipboard manager**
2. Long-press a clip you want to keep
3. Tap **📌 Pin**
4. Pinned clips stay at the top and won't be auto-deleted

### Delete a Clip

1. Open the **clipboard manager**
2. **Long-press** the clip you want to remove
3. Tap **🗑️ Delete** from the menu
4. Confirm deletion

### Delete Multiple Clips

1. Open the **clipboard manager**
2. Long-press any clip
3. Tap **Select Multiple**
4. Tap all clips you want to delete
5. Tap **🗑️ Delete Selected**

### Clear All Clipboard History

1. Open the **clipboard manager**
2. Tap the **⋮ menu icon** (three dots)
3. Select **Clear All**
4. Confirm - this deletes all unpinned clips

<AccordionItem title="Clipboard Categories">

Flashboard organizes clips into smart categories:

### All Clips
- Shows your complete clipboard history
- Most recent clips appear first

### Pinned
- Clips you've manually pinned
- Never auto-delete
- Perfect for frequently used text

### Links
- Automatically detected URLs
- Quick access to copied links

### Text
- Plain text clips only
- Filters out links and special content

</AccordionItem>

<AccordionItem title="Edit Clips Before Pasting">

1. Open the **clipboard manager**
2. Long-press a clip
3. Tap **✏️ Edit**
4. Modify the text
5. Tap **Save** - the updated version is saved
6. Tap **Paste** to insert the edited clip

</AccordionItem>

<AccordionItem title="Clipboard Settings">

Customize clipboard behavior:

1. Open **Flashboard app** > **Settings** > **Clipboard**
2. Adjust these options:

**Enable Clipboard History**
- Toggle to turn clipboard saving on/off

**Auto-Save Copies**
- Automatically save everything you copy (recommended)

**History Limit**
- Choose how many clips to store (10, 25, 50, 100, unlimited)

**Auto-Delete After**
- Set when old clips are removed (24 hours, 7 days, 30 days, never)

**Sensitive Content Filter**
- Option to exclude passwords and credit card numbers

**Show Categories**
- Enable/disable smart categorization

</AccordionItem>

<AccordionItem title="Privacy & Security">

### What Gets Saved?
- Text copied from any app
- URLs and links
- Text from screenshots (if OCR enabled)

### What Doesn't Get Saved?
- Passwords (if sensitive filter is enabled)
- Credit card numbers (automatically detected and excluded)
- Banking app content (respects app security policies)

### Clear Clipboard Data
- Clips are stored locally on your device only
- Not synced to cloud or shared with Flashboard servers
- Delete anytime from clipboard manager

### Disable Clipboard History

1. Open **Flashboard app** > **Settings** > **Clipboard**
2. Toggle **Enable Clipboard History** off
3. Optionally choose **Clear Existing History**

</AccordionItem>

### Quick Access

**Quick Access:**
- Swipe up from spacebar to open clipboard (if gestures enabled)
- Use voice command "Open clipboard" (if voice commands enabled)

**Smart Suggestions:**
- Flashboard shows relevant clipboard items above the keyboard

### Tips for Power Users

1. **Pin Frequently Used Text**

   - Quick access without searching

2. **Use Categories**
   - Swipe between categories to find clips faster
   - Link category perfect for sharing URLs

3. **Edit and Reuse**
   - Edit clips to create templates
   - Copy-paste with modifications

4. **Organize with Snippets**
   - Create reusable text snippets
   - See [Keyboard Signature](/features/keyboard-signature) for automated signatures

5. **Regular Cleanup**
   - Clear old clips weekly
   - Keep only what you need for better performance

<AccordionItem title="Troubleshooting">

### Clipboard not saving copies?
- Go to **Settings** > **Clipboard** and enable **Auto-Save Copies**
- Check if Flashboard has clipboard permission
- Restart Flashboard app

### Clips disappearing too quickly?
- Adjust **Auto-Delete After** setting
- Pin important clips to prevent deletion
- Increase **History Limit**

### Can't see clipboard icon?
- Check **Settings** > **Toolbar** > ensure **Clipboard** is enabled
- Try restarting the Flashboard keyboard
- Update to the latest version

### Clipboard showing sensitive info?
- Enable **Sensitive Content Filter** in settings
- Manually delete sensitive clips
- Use **Clear All** to remove all data
