# Data Safety & Privacy

Your privacy is our top priority. Flashboard is designed with security and transparency at its core.

<Accordion>
<AccordionItem title="What We Never Collect">
**Flashboard does NOT collect:**

1. **Keystrokes or typed content**
   - We never log, record, or transmit what you type
   - Your messages, passwords, and personal information stay private
   - No keystroke logging, ever

2. **Personal identifiable information (PII)**
   - No names, email addresses, or phone numbers
   - No contacts or address book data
   - No location tracking

3. **Clipboard content details**
   - While clipboard history is saved locally on your device, content is never sent to our servers
   - All clipboard data stays on your device

4. **Financial information**
   - No credit card numbers, banking details, or payment info
   - Sensitive content is automatically filtered from clipboard
</AccordionItem>

<AccordionItem title="What We Collect (Only With Your Permission)">
**Optional data collection requires explicit consent:**

### 1. Anonymous Usage Statistics
   - **What:** General feature usage (e.g., "emoji panel opened")
   - **Why:** To improve app performance and user experience
   - **Control:** Can be disabled in Settings > Privacy > Usage Statistics

### 2. Crash Reports
   - **What:** Technical diagnostic data when app crashes
   - **Why:** To fix bugs and improve stability
   - **What's included:** Device type, Android version, app version, crash location
   - **What's NOT included:** Your actual typed content or personal data
   - **Control:** Can be disabled in Settings > Privacy > Crash Reports

### 3. Language Preferences
   - **What:** Selected keyboard languages
   - **Why:** To improve language support and autocorrect
   - **Storage:** Stored locally on your device only
</AccordionItem>

<AccordionItem title="How Your Data is Protected">
### On-Device Storage

All personal data is stored locally:
- **Clipboard history** - Saved only on your device
- **Custom themes** - Stored in local app data
- **Keyboard signatures** - Saved locally
- **Dictionary & learned words** - Device-only storage
- **Settings & preferences** - Local storage only

### No Cloud Sync (By Default)

Flashboard does not sync your data to the cloud unless you explicitly:
- Enable backup features (optional)
- Sign in to a Flashboard account (optional)
- Request to sync settings across devices (optional)

### Encryption

- Local data is protected by Android's app-level encryption
- Communication with our servers (for updates) uses HTTPS encryption
- Backup data (if enabled) is encrypted end-to-end

### Third-Party Services

Flashboard may integrate with third-party services only with your permission:
- **Google Play Services** - For app updates and analytics (if enabled)
- **Firebase** - For optional crash reporting (if enabled)
- **Ad networks** - Only in free version, no personalized ads without consent
</AccordionItem>

<AccordionItem title="Your Privacy Controls">
### Manage Permissions

Control what Flashboard can access:

1. Go to **Android Settings** > **Apps** > **Flashboard**
2. Tap **Permissions**
3. Enable/disable:
   - **Microphone** - For voice input (optional)
   - **Storage** - For custom themes and backups (optional)
   - **Contacts** - For name suggestions (optional)

### Disable Data Collection

1. Open **Flashboard app** > **Settings** > **Privacy**
2. Toggle off:
   - **Usage Statistics**
   - **Crash Reports**
   - **Personalized Suggestions** (uses local learning only)

### Clear Local Data

Delete all local data from your device:

1. Open **Flashboard app** > **Settings** > **Privacy**
2. Choose what to clear:
   - **Clear Clipboard History** - Deletes all saved clips
   - **Clear Learned Words** - Removes custom dictionary
   - **Reset Settings** - Restores default settings
   - **Delete All Data** - Complete reset (requires confirmation)

Or from Android Settings:
1. **Settings** > **Apps** > **Flashboard**
2. Tap **Storage**
3. Tap **Clear Data** or **Clear Cache**
</AccordionItem>

<AccordionItem title="Data Retention">
- **Local data:** Retained until you delete it or uninstall the app
- **Usage statistics:** Anonymized and retained for 90 days
- **Crash reports:** Retained for 90 days, then automatically deleted
- **Account data:** Deleted within 30 days of account deletion (if you have an account)
</AccordionItem>

<AccordionItem title="Compliance & Standards">
Flashboard complies with:
- **GDPR** (General Data Protection Regulation) - European Union
- **CCPA** (California Consumer Privacy Act) - California, USA
- **Google Play Store** data safety requirements
- **Android** privacy guidelines
</AccordionItem>

<AccordionItem title="Children's Privacy">
Flashboard does not knowingly collect data from children under 13:
- No account required for basic keyboard functionality
- No targeted advertising to children
- Compliant with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act)
</AccordionItem>

<AccordionItem title="Third-Party Keyboard Security">
As an Android keyboard (Input Method Editor), Flashboard:

✅ **Does:**
- Follow Android security best practices
- Request only necessary permissions
- Isolate itself from other apps via Android's security model
- Use Android's secure input framework

❌ **Does NOT:**
- Have access to other apps' data
- Run in the background when keyboard is not active
- Communicate with other apps on your device
- Bypass Android's security protections
</AccordionItem>

<AccordionItem title="Data Breach Protocol">
In the unlikely event of a data breach:
1. We will notify affected users within 72 hours
2. We will report to relevant authorities as required by law
3. We will provide detailed information about what data was affected
4. We will offer guidance on protective measures
</AccordionItem>

<AccordionItem title="Questions About Privacy?">
**Review our full privacy policy:**
- Visit [flashboard.app/privacy](https://flashboard.app/privacy)
- Or open **Flashboard app** > **Settings** > **About** > **Privacy Policy**

**Contact us:**
- Email: privacy@flashboard.app
- Submit a request in the app: **Settings** > **Help & Support** > **Contact Us**

**Request your data:**
- Email privacy@flashboard.app with subject "Data Request"
- We'll provide any data we have associated with your account within 30 days

**Delete your account & data:**
- Open **Flashboard app** > **Settings** > **Account** > **Delete Account**
- Or email privacy@flashboard.app with subject "Account Deletion"
</AccordionItem>

<AccordionItem title="Best Practices for Your Privacy">
1. **Review permissions regularly**
   - Only grant permissions you're comfortable with
   - Revoke unused permissions

2. **Keep Flashboard updated**
   - Updates include security improvements
   - Enable auto-updates in Play Store

3. **Use secure lock screen**
   - Protect your device with PIN, pattern, or biometric lock
   - This protects locally stored data

4. **Be cautious with sensitive info**
   - Disable clipboard history when handling passwords
   - Clear clipboard regularly in sensitive contexts

5. **Read what you agree to**
   - Review privacy policy updates
   - Understand feature permissions before enabling
</AccordionItem>
