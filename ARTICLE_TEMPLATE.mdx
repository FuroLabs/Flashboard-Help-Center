## Styling Guidelines

### Left-Accent List Styling
The CSS automatically applies a subtle gray vertical line to the left of ordered lists (`<ol>`). This creates the clean, minimalist look:
- Lists are numbered (1., 2., 3.)
- A 3px gray border appears on the left
- Proper padding ensures readability
- Works automatically in accordion content

### When to Use Accordions
✅ **Use accordions for:**
- Step-by-step instructions
- How-to sections
- Feature explanations
- Settings guides
- Troubleshooting sections

❌ **Don't use accordions for:**
- Brief introductions (first paragraph)
- Simple bullet lists
- Next Steps links
- Very short sections (< 3 lines)

### Section Organization
1. **Title & Introduction** - Always outside accordions
2. **Overview** - Brief context (optional, outside accordions)
3. **Main Content** - Wrapped in `<Accordion>` component
4. **Next Steps** - Always outside accordions at the end

### Accordion Best Practices
- Each `<AccordionItem>` needs a clear, descriptive `title` attribute
- First accordion item opens by default
- Only one accordion item is open at a time
- Keep accordion titles concise (3-7 words)
- Group related content within the same accordion item
